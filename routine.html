Your daily ritual with GLOWFINITY

General skincare guidance is separate from verified product steps. Product-specific instructions below come from the printed labels; the rest is widely-accepted general routine information.

1
Gentle cleanse (AM + PM)

Face wash: wet skin, massage a small amount ~30 seconds, rinse. Label suggests 1–2× daily. Soap: lather, massage gently, rinse thoroughly.

2
Patch-test new products

Apply a small amount behind the ear or on the jawline; wait 24 hours. If redness, itching or burning occurs, do not continue.

3
Moisturise (general)

While skin is slightly damp, apply a moisturiser suited to your skin type. This is general guidance, not a product claim.

4
Sunscreen in daytime (general)

As a general consideration, use broad-spectrum sun protection during the day and reapply per its own label.

Using the Face Wash — per label

  • Wet skin with warm water
  • Massage Crystal Glow / GLOWFINITY face wash
  • Leave briefly, rinse with cold water
  • Morning + evening use suggested on pack

Using the Soap — cautious steps

  • Wet skin, build a soft lather
  • Cleanse gently; avoid eyes
  • Rinse fully and pat dry
  • Discontinue if irritation occurs
